Research

At the moment, my research directions are:

  • Representation and latent-prediction models for dynamical systems. I train JEPA-style latent-prediction models on families of nonlinear dynamical systems and measure, quantitatively, what they actually internalize about the underlying physics — reading structure directly off the learned embedding manifold rather than relying on qualitative probes. The models recover a smooth, low-dimensional manifold of the governing parameter, reproducible across seeds and across renderings that share no colors, which separates what is learned about the dynamics from the surface appearance.
  • Efficient, data-centric language models for low-resource languages. I built a large-scale Romanian corpus and full training pipeline and trained small decoder-only transformers from scratch, releasing quantized builds that run in real time on constrained hardware. The work spans the whole data-centric stack: corpus construction, tokenizer design, training, and on-device deployment.
  • Computational and statistical foundations of ML. Across both directions I care about models that are small, interpretable, and calibrated, grounded in numerical methods, Monte Carlo, and statistical modelling — and about measuring what a learned representation genuinely captures rather than taking it on faith.
