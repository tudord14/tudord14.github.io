Research Interests

I'm broadly interested in machine learning and its computational and statistical foundations. Some directions I care about:

  • Representation learning & self-supervised models. Encoders, latent-prediction architectures, and joint-embedding methods; and, more generally, understanding what these models actually learn and how their representations are structured.
  • Theoretical & computational machine learning. The mathematical side of learning: optimization, numerical methods, Monte Carlo, and statistical modelling; and models that are small, interpretable, and grounded in solid computational foundations.
